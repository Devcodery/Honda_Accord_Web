Cargo Area Light Test/Replacement

1.Disconnect the 2P connectors (A) from the convenience facility garnish (B).

 

2.Remove the lens (A) from the convenience facility garnish.

BULB: 5 W 

 

3.Check for continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, check the bulb. If the bulb is OK, replace the cargo area light.
