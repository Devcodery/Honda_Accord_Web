Charging System Indicator Circuit Troubleshooting

1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

Does the charging system indicator come on?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Go to 3.


2.Start the engine.

Does the charging system indicator go off?


YES - Charging system indicator circuit is OK. Go to the Alternator and Regulator Circuit Troubleshooting.n


NO - Go to 3.


3.Do the gauge control module self-diagnosis function procedure.

Does the charging system indicator flash?


YES - Go to 4.


NO - Replace the gauge control module.n


4.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

5.Disconnect the alternator 4P connector.

6.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

7.Measure the voltage between alternator 4P connector terminal No. 1 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 8.


NO - Check for a blown No. 18 (15 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box. If the fuse is OK, repair open in the wire between the alternator and under-dash fuse/relay box.n


8.Turn the ignition switch OFF, and wait at least 30 seconds.

9.Disconnect engine control module (ECM) connector No. 1 (96P).

10.Check for continuity between ECM connector No. 1 terminal No. 35 and alternator 4P connector terminal No. 3.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 11.


NO - Repair an open in the wire between the alternator and the ECM.n


11.Check for continuity between ECM connector No. 1 terminal No. 35 and body ground.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Repair a short in the wire between the alternator and the ECM.n


NO - Go to the Alternator and Regulator Circuit Troubleshooting.n
