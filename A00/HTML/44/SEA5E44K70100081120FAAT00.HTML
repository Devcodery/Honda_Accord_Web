DTC Troubleshooting: 12, 14, 16, 18

DTC 12, 14, 16, 18:  Wheel Sensor (Electrical Noise/Intermittent Interruption)



NOTE: If the ABS indicator comes on because of electrical noise, the indicator will go off when you drive the vehicle over 30 km/h (19 mph) and the noise is gone.

1.Inspect the appropriate wheel sensor and magnetic encoder or pulser.

DTC Appropriate Wheel Sensor 
12 Right-front 
14 Left-front 
16 Right-rear 
18 Left-rear 


Are they OK?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Clean and reinstall or replace the appropriate wheel sensor or magnetic encoder or pulser.n


2.Disconnect the ABS control unit 25P connector.

3.Check for continuity between the appropriate wheel sensor +B terminal and other wheel sensor +B terminals (see table).

DTC Appropriate
Terminal 		Other Terminals 
12 FR +B:
No. 2 		No. 12 No. 6 No. 14 
14 FL +B:
No. 12 		No. 2 No. 6 No. 14 
16 RR +B:
No. 6 		No. 2 No. 12 No. 14 
18 RL +B:
No. 14 		No. 2 No. 12 No. 6 

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Repair short in the wire between the appropriate wheel sensor and the other wheel sensor.n


NO - Go to 4.

4.Substitute a known-good wheel sensor fot the appropriate wheel sensor.

DTC Appropriate Wheel Sensor 
12 Right-front 
14 Left-front 
16 Right-rear 
18 Left-rear 


5.Clear the DTC using the HDS.

6.Disconnect the HDS from 16P DLC.

7.Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II) again.

8.Test-drive the vehicle.

Does the ABS indicator come on?


YES - Check for loose terminals in the ABS control unit 25P connector. If necessary, substitute a known-good ABS modulator-control unit, and recheck.n


NO - Replace the original wheel sensor.n
