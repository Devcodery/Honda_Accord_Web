DTC Troubleshooting: 84

DTC 84:  VSA Sensor Neutral Position


1.Check the DTC.

Is another DTC indicated?


YES - Do the appropriate troubleshooting for the DTC.n


NO - Go to 2.


2.Do the VSA sensor neutral position memorization.

3.Clear the DTC using the HDS.

4.Disconnect the HDS from 16P DLC.

5.Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II) again

Does the VSA indicator come on, and is DTC 84 indicated?


YES - Replace the VSA modulator-control unit.n


NO - The system is OK at this time.n
