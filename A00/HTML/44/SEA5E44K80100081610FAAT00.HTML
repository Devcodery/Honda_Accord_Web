DTC Troubleshooting: 61, 62

DTC 61, 62:  High/Low Voltage


1.Clear the DTC using the HDS.

2.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

Does the ABS indicator come on?


YES - Go to 3.


NO - The system is OK at this time.n


3.Verify the DTC.

Is DTC 61 or 62 indicated?


YES - Check the battery and the charging system.n


NO - Do the appropriate troubleshooting for the DTC indicated.n
