DTC Troubleshooting: 21, 22, 23, 24

DTC 21, 22:  Magnetic Encoder (front)
  
  


1.Clear the DTC using the HDS.

2.Disconnect the HDS from the 16P DLC.

3.Test-drive the vehicle at 30 km/h (19 mph) or more.

Does the ABS indicator come on, and are DTCs 21, 22, 23, and/or 24 indicated?


YES - Go to 4.


NO - The system is OK at this time.n


4.Check the appropriate wheel sensor/encoder or pulser (see table).

DTC Appropriate Encoder or Pulser 
21 Right-front 
22 Left-front 
23 Right-rear 
24 Left-rear 


Is the encoder or pulser OK?


YES - Check for loose terminals in the ABS control unit 25P connector. If necessary, substitute a known-good ABS modulator-control unit, and recheck.n


NO - Clean and reinstall or replace the magnetic encoder or pulser.n
