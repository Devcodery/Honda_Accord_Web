Low Fuel Indicator Test

1.Do the gauge drive circuit check.

  • If the low fuel indicator flashes, go to 2.
  • If the low fuel indicator does not flush, replace the gauge control module assembly.

2.Perform the multiplex integrated control system troubleshooting test mode A.

  • If any DTCs are indicated, do the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.
  • If no DTC's are indicated, go to 3.

3.Perform fuel gauge sending unit test.
