Interior Trim Removal/Installation - Tailgate Area

NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Use the handy remover set, or equivalent when prying components.
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.

1.Remove the tailgate upper trim (A).

-1 Release the hooks (B) from the left tailgate side trim (C) and right tailgate side trim (D).
-2 Pull the tailgate upper trim back by hand to detach the clips.

 

2.Remove the left tailgate side trim (A) and the right tailgate side trim (B).

-1 Pull out the top of the trim to release the pins (C) from the grommet (D) and detach the clips (E).
-2 Release the hooks (F) and tabs (G) from the tailgate lower trim panel (H).

 

3.Remove the tailgate lower trim panel (A).

-1 Pry up on the lid (B), remove the self-tapping ET screw (C) using a T25 Torx bit, then remove the tailgate pull pocket (D). If equipped, disconnect the power tailgate close switch connector (E).
-2 Release the clips (F).
-3 Pull out the trim panel to detach the clips (G, H).

 

4.Install the trim in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Replace any damaged clips.
  • If the threads on a pull pocket screw are worn out, use an oversized self-tapping ET screw, P/N90137-S1A-E01, made specifically for this application.
  • If equipped, make sure the power tailgate close switch connector is plugged in properly, and check the power tailgate operation.
