M/T Clutch Housing Mainshaft Bearing and Oil Seal Replacement

Special Tools Required
Oil seal driver (  07JAD-PL90100)
 

Adjustable bearing puller, 20-40 mm (  07JAC-PH80100)
 
Bearing remover shaft (  07JAC-PH80200)
 
Slide hammer (  07741-0010201)
 
Attachment, 42 x 47 mm (  07746-0010300)
 
Driver (  07749-0010000)
 


1.Remove the differential assembly.

2.Remove the ball bearing (A) from the clutch housing (B) using the special tools.

 

3.Remove the oil seal (A) from the clutch side. Be careful when removing the seal so the clutch housing is not damaged.

 

4.Drive the new oil seal in from the transmission side using the special tools.

 

5.Drive the new ball bearing (A) in from the transmission side using the special tools.
