Rear Inner Fender Replacement

NOTE: Take care not to scratch the body.

1.Remove the rear inner fender (A).

-1 On the front of the wheel arch, remove the screws (B) securing the side sill panel (C) and rear inner fender and the clip (D) securing the rear inner fender and middle floor under cover (E).
-2 On the rear bumper (F), remove the screws (G).
-3 From the wheel arch, remove the screw (H), clips (I, J), and grommet (K).
-4 Pull the rear inner fender out.

 

2.Install the inner fender in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Replace any damaged clips.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
