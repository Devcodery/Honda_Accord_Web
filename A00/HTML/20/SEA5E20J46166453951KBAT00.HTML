Rear Seat-back Cover Replacement

NOTE:
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.

1.Remove the seat-back.

2.Remove these items:

  • Armrest
  • Headrest
  • Center headrest

3.Remove the screws, then remove the center belt guide.

 

4.From the front of the seat-back, detach the clips, then remove the armrest back panel (A).

 

5.Release the hooks (A, B) from the seat-back frame (C).

 

6.Release the hook (A) and fastner (B), and unzip the seat-back cover (C). The left seat-back is shown; the right seat-back is similar.

 

7.Release all the clips (A), and fold back the seat-back cover (B).

Left seat-back 

Right seat-back 

8.Pull out the seat-back pad (A) from the frame (B), then pull out the headrest guides (C) while pinching the end of the guides, and remove them.

 

9.Remove the seat-back cover and pad from the seat-back frame.

10.Pull back the edge of the seat-back cover (A) all the way around, and release the clips (B), then remove the seat-back cover.

Left seat-back 

Right seat-back 

11.Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • To prevent wrinkles, make sure the material is stretched evenly over the pad before securing the clips.
  • Replace any clips you removed with new ones. Install them with commercially available upholstery ring pliers.
