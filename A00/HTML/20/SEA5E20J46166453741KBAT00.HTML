Rear Seat Side Bolster Cover Replacement

1.Remove the seat side bolster.

2.Release all the hook strips (A), and fold back the seat side bolster cover (B), and release the cover from the hook (C), and release the staples (D) fastening the cover, to remove it.

 

3.Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • To prevent wrinkles, make sure the material is stretched evenly over the pad before securing the hook strips and staples.
  • Fasten the cover with new staples (MAX 1005F) securely.
