Audio System Symptom Troubleshooting - CD does not eject

CD does not eject


1.Check to see if the CD disc is ejected correctly by pushing the EJECT button.

Is it normal?


YES - Operation is normal.n


NO - Go to 2.


2.Check to see if it is ejected with some binding or not ejected at all.

Is it ejected with some binding?


YES - Go to 3.


NO - Go to the flow ''Power switch will not turn ON''.n


3.Replace the disc and recheck.

Does the problem continue?


YES - Go to 4.


NO - Faulty disc.n


4.Remove the audio assembly from dashboard and remove the audio unit, and check for continuity on the EJECT button using the connectors on the front printed circuit board.

 


Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Faulty front printed circuit.n

5.Remove the audio-HVAC display module unit. Check the printed circuit board and audio unit for assembling integrity.

Are they assembled correctly?


YES - Faulty audio unit.n


NO - Reinstall the printed circuit board onto the audio unit, and recheck the problem.n
