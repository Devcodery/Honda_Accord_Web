DTC Troubleshooting: E3-11, F1-11, F2-11, F3-11, F4-11

DTC E3-11, F1-11, F2-11, F3-11, F4-11: 
  


The SRS unit must be replaced after any airbags and/or tensioners have deployed.n

NOTE: DTC E3-11 is set if the system triggered a passenger's side airbag deployment but the airbag was prevented from deploying by the OPDS. Replace the right side impact sensor (first).
