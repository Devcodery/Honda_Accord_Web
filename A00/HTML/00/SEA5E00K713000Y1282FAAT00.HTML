DTC Troubleshooting: B1282

DTC B1282:  Windshield Wiper Switch INT (AUTO) Position Circuit Malfunction


1.Clear the DTCs using the HDS.

2.Cycle the ignition switch to OFF, and then back ON.

3.Turn the windshield wiper switch to INT (AUTO) position for 2 seconds.

4.Check the DTCs by selecting the DTCs MENU from the HDS.

Is DTC B1282 indicated?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Intermittent failure, the windshield wiper/washer switch and the combination switch control unit (LHD type) are OK at this time.n


5.Ignition switch OFF. Disconnect the wiper/washer switch 12P connector.

6.Turn the windshield wiper switch in INT (AUTO) position.

7.Check for continuity between the wiper/washer switch 12P connector No. 4 and No.12 connector.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Replace the  combination control unit.n


NO - Replace the  wiper/washer switch.n
