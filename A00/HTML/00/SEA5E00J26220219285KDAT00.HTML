Dashboard Center Pocket Removal/Installation

SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations LHD, SRS component locations RHD and the precautions and procedures before performing repairs or service.

NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.

1.Remove the center holder.

2.Remove the center pocket (A).

-1 Remove the screws (B).
-2 Open the lid, then pull the pocket back by hand to detach the clips (C).
-3 Close the lid, then remove the pocket.


Without navigation system and/or premium sound system 

With navigation system and/or premium sound system 

3.Install the pocket in the reverse order of removal, and make sure the holders (D) are installed onto the pins (E) properly (without navigation system and/or premium sound system).
