Headlight Adjuster Unit Troubleshooting

NOTE: Before testing, check for a blown No. 20 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.

1.Disconnect the 3P connector (A) from the headlight adjuster unit (B).

 

2.Check for continuity between the No. 3 terminal and body ground.

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 3.


NO - Check for these problems:

  • Repair open in the BLK wire between the headlight adjuster unit and body ground.n
  • Poor ground (G201, G301).n


3.Check for voltage between the No. 1 terminal and body ground.

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 4.


NO - Repair open in the YEL/GRN wire between the headlight adjuster unit and under-dash fuse/relay box.n

4.Using an ohmmeter, measure resistance between the No. 2 terminal and body ground in position 0 of the headlight adjuster switch.

Is there about 730 W?


YES - Check for frozen, stuck or improperly installed the headlight adjuster unit. If the mechanical check is OK, replace the headlight adjuster unit.n


NO - Check for these problems:

  • Poor ground (G501).n
  • An open in the BLU/BLK wire between the headlight adjuster unit and headlight adjuster switch.n
  • A faulty headlight adjuster switch.n
