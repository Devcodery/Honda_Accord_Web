DTC Troubleshooting: B1077

DTC B1077:  Windshield Wiper (As) Signal Error


1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

2.Turn the windshield wiper switch ON.

Does the reception desk wiper operate?


YES - Go to 4.


NO - Go to 3.


3.Check the No. 11 (30 A) fuse and wiper relay in the under-dash fuse/relay box.

4.Clear the DTCs using the HDS.

5.Check the ON/OFF information of the FR WIPER (AS) in the DATA LIST.

Does the information indicator ON and OFF Alternately as the wipers pass the PARK position?


YES - Replace the relay control module.n


NO - Go to 6.

6.Check for continuity between the No. 4 [No. 5] terminal of the windshield wiper motor 5P connector and No. 5 terminal of the under-hood fuse/relay box 5P connector J.
[ ]: RHD type

 


Is there continuity?


YES - Replace the windshield wiper motor.n


NO - Repair an open in the WHT wire.n
