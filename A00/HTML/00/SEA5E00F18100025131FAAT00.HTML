EPS Indicator Circuit Troubleshooting

1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

2.Start the engine, and watch the EPS indicator.

Does the EPS indicator come on?


YES - If the EPS indicator comes on and goes off, it's OK. If the EPS indicator stays on or blinks, go to 11.


NO - Go to 3.


3.Turn the ignition switch OFF, then ON (II) again, and watch the brake system indicator.

Does the brake system indicator come on?


YES - Go to 4.


NO - Repair open in the indicator power source circuit:n

  • Blown No. 21 (7.5 A) fuse.
  • Open in the wire between the No. 21 (7.5 A) fuse and gauge control module.
  • Open circuit inside the under-dash fuse/relay box.

4.Connect gauge control module connector A (10P) terminal No. 10 to body ground with a jumper wire.

 

Does the EPS indicator come on?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Go to gauge control module troubleshooting.n


5.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

6.Disconnect EPS control unit connector C (13P).

7.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

8.Connect EPS control unit connector C (13P) terminal No. 11 and body ground with a jumper wire.

 

Does the EPS indicator come on?


YES - Go to 9.


NO - Repair open in the wire between gauge control module and EPS control unit.n

9.Check for continuity between body ground and EPS control unit connector C (13P) terminals No. 1 and No. 5 individually.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 10.


NO - Repair open in the wires between EPS control unit and body ground (G503).n


10.Measure the voltage between EPS control unit connector C (13P) terminal No. 6 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Check for loose terminals and poor connectors at the EPS control unit and gauge control module connectors. If necessary, substitute a known-good EPS control unit and recheck.n


NO - Repair open in the wire between No. 21 ( 7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and EPS control unit.n

11.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

12.Disconnect EPS control unit connector C (13P).

13.Disconnect the connectors from the following units.

  • ECM/PCM connector E (31P)
  • ABS control unit
  • SRS control unit

14.Check for continuity between EPS control unit connector C (13P) terminal No. 12 and body ground.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Repair short to body ground in the SCS circuit.n


NO - Go to 15.


15.Connect EPS control unit connector C (13P).

16.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

17.Measure the voltage between data link connector (16P) terminal No. 9 and body ground.

 

Is there about 6 V?


YES - Go to 18.


NO - Repair open in the wire between data link connector (16P) and EPS control unit.n


18.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

19.Reconnect all connectors.

20.Disconnect EPS control unit connector C (13P).

21.Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and start the engine.

Does the EPS indicator go off?


YES - Go to 22.


NO - Repair short to body ground in the wire between gauge control module and EPS control unit.n


22.Troubleshoot the gauge control module.

Is it normal?


YES - Check for loose terminals and poor connections at EPS control unit and gauge control module connectors. If necessary substitute a known-good EPS control unit and recheck.n


NO -  Replace the gauge control module.n
