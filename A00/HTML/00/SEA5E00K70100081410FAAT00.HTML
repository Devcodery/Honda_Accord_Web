DTC Troubleshooting: 41, 42, 43, 44

DTC 41, 42, 43, 44:  Wheel Lock


1.Test-drive the vehicle, and check for brake drag by duplicating city driving at speeds over 50 km/h (30 mph). Use the brakes often.

Do the brakes drag?


YES - Repair the brake drag.n


NO - Go to 2.


2.Check the installation of the appropriate wheel sensor.

DTC Appropriate Wheel Sensor 
41 Right-front 
42 Left-front 
43 Right-rear 
44 Left-rear 


Is it correct?


YES - If the DTC does not reappear, the most probable cause for the DTC is that the vehicle might have lost grip force on slippery road surface and spun around.n


NO - Reinstall or replace the wheel sensor.n
