DTC Troubleshooting: B1325

DTC B1325:  Intermittent Wiper Functions But Auto Wiper Does Not Function


1.Check the crack or damage of the windshield glass.

Is there any crack or damage?


YES - Replace the windshield glass.n


NO - Go to 2.


2.Check the rain sensor installation.

Is the rain sensor installed properly?


YES - Go to 3.


NO - Reinstall the rain sensor, and recheck.n


3.Check the connection of the rain sensor 5P connector.

Is the connector connected properly?


YES - Replace the  rain sensor.n


NO - Reconnect the 5P connector, and recheck.n
