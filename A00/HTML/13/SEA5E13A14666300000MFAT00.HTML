Cylinder Head Rocker Arm and Shaft Disassembly/Reassembly

NOTE:

  • Identify parts as they are removed to ensure reinstallation in original location.
  • Inspect the rocker arm shaft and rocker arms.
  • The rocker arms must be installed in the same positions if reused.
  • When removing or installing the rocker arm assembly, do not remove the camshaft holder bolts. The bolts will keep the holders and rocker arms on the shaft.
  • Prior to reassembling, clean all the parts in solvent, dry them, and apply lubricant to any contact points.
  • Bundle the intake rocker arms with rubber bands to keep them together as a set.
  • When replacing the VTEC rocker arm assembly, remove the fastening hardware from the new VTEC rocker arm assembly.
