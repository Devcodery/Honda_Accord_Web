DTC Troubleshooting: 83

DTC 83:  ECM/PCM


1.Check for other DTCs.

Is DTC 86 indicated?


YES - Do the troubleshooting for the DTC 86.n


NO - Go to 2.


2.Clear the DTC using the HDS.

3.Disconnect the HDS from the 16P DLC.

4.Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II) again.

5.Test-drive the vehicle.

Do the VSA and VSA activation indicator come on, and is DTC 83 indicated?


YES - Go to 6.


NO - Intermittent failure; the system is OK at this time.n


6.Check the PGM-FI system.

Does the MIL indicator come on or is the ECM/PCM's DTC indicated?


YES - Do the applicable troubleshooting for the ECM/PCM.n


NO - Go to 7. (A/T)
Check for loose terminals at the ECM connectors, and go to 8. (M/T)


7.Check the A/T gear position indicator.

Does the D indicator come on while neutral position (N) selected or is PCM's DTC indicated?


YES - Do the applicable troubleshooting for the PCM.n


NO - Check for loose terminals at the PCM connectors, and go to 8.

8.Clear the DTC using the HDS.

9.Disconnect the HDS from the 16P DLC.

10.Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II) again.

11.Test-drive the vehicle.

Is DTC 83 indicated and no ECM/PCM's DTC stored?


YES - Replace the VSA modulator-control unit.n


NO - The system is OK at this time.n
