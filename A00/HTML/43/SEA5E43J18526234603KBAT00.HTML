Rear Inner Fender Replacement

NOTE: Take care not to scratch the body.

1.Remove the rear inner fender (A).

-1 Remove the bolt (B) from the wheel arch.
-2 On the front of the wheel arch, remove the screws (C) securing the side sill panel (D) and rear inner fender.
-3 On the rear bumper (E), remove the screws (C, F).
-4 From the wheel arch, remove the clips (G, H, I).
-5 Pull the rear inner fender out.

 

2.Install the inner fender in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Replace any damaged clips.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
