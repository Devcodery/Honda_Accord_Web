Power Tailgate Touch Sensor Replacement

Power Tailgate


NOTE:
  • Use the handy remover set, or equivalent when prying components.
  • Take care not to scratch the tailgate and not to bend the bracket.

1.Disconnect the negative cable from the battery.

NOTE: If you remove the touch sensor with the battery negative cable still in plugged, the vehicle falls into the fail-safe mode.

2.Remove the maintenance lid (A).

 

3.Remove the upper tailgate touch sensor (A).

-1 Disconnect the sensor connector (B).
-2 Remove the mounting bolt (C), screws (D), and clip (E).
-3 Remove the upper tailgate touch sensor and grommet (F) from the tailgate, then pull the harness out.

 

4.Remove the lower tailgate touch sensor (A).

-1 Disconnect the sensor connector (B).
-2 Remove the mounting bolt (C) and clip (D).
-3 Remove the lower tailgate touch sensor and grommet (E) from the tailgate, then pull the harness out.

 

5.Install the sensor in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Make sure the connectors are plugged in properly.
  • Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  • Reset the clock.
  • Perform the power window control unit reset procedure.
  • Check the tailgate touch sensor operation.
