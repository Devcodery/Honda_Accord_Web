ATF Cooler Hose Replacement

1.Slide the ATF cooler hoses (A) on the ATF filter (B) to the filter housing wall.

 

2.Secure the hoses with the clip (C) at 6-8 mm (0.2-0.3 in.) (D) from the filter housing wall.

3.Slide the ATF cooler hoses (E) over the ATF cooler lines (F).

4.Secure the hoses with the clips (G) at 6-8 mm (0.2-0.3 in.) (H) from the hose ends, and secure the hoses at the ATF cooler with the clips (I) at 2- 4 mm (0.1-0.2 in.) (J) from the hose ends.

5.Install the hoses on the clamps (K) at the marks (L).
