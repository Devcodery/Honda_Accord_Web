A/T Interlock System - Shift Lock Solenoid Replacement

NOTE: The illustration shows LHD model; RHD model is similar.

1.Remove the center console.

2.Disconnect the shift lock solenoid connector.

3.Remove the shift lock release lever (A).

 

4.Remove the shift lock solenoid.

5.Install the shift lock solenoid plunger (B) and plunger spring (C) in the new shift lock solenoid (D).

6.Install the new shift lock solenoid by aligning the joint of the shift lock solenoid plunger with the tip of the shift lock stop (E).

7.Install the shift lock release lever.

8.Connect the shift lock solenoid connector, and install the connector on the connector bracket.

9.Install the center console.
