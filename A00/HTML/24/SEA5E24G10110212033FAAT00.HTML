A/C Condenser Fan High Speed Circuit Troubleshooting

NOTE:
  • Do not use this troubleshooting procedure if the radiator fan and/or the A/C compressor is inoperative. Refer to the symptom troubleshooting index.
  • Before performing symptom troubleshooting, check for powertrain DTCs.

1.Check the No. 30 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.

Is the fuse OK?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Replace the fuse, and recheck.n


2.Remove the fan control relay from the multi-relay box, and test it.

Is the relay OK?


YES - Go to 3.


NO - Replace the fan control relay.n


3.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

4.Measure the voltage between the No. 5 terminal of the fan control relay 5P socket and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Go to 17.


5.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

6.Check for continuity between the No. 2 terminal of the fan control relay 5P socket and body ground.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 7.


NO - Check for an open in the wire between the fan control relay and body ground. If the wire is OK, check for poor ground at G302.n

7.Remove A/C diode B from the left side (LHD) or the right side (RHD) of the dashboard.

8.Check for current flow in both directions between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals of A/C diode B.

 

Is there current flow in only one direction?


YES - Go to 9.


NO - Replace A/C diode B.n


9.Make sure the engine coolant temperature is below 93 °C (199 °F).

10.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

11.Measure the voltage between the No. 2 terminal of A/C diode B 2P connector and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 12.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the A/C condenser fan relay and A/C diode B.n

12.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

13.Reinstall the A/C diode B.

14.Disconnect climate control unit connector B (16P).

15.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

16.Measure the voltage between the No. 12 terminal of climate control unit connector B (16P) and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Check for loose wires or poor connections at climate control unit B (16P) and at A/C diode B 2P connector. If the connections are good, substitute a known-good climate control unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original  climate control unit.n


NO - Repair open in the wire between A/C diode B and the climate control unit.n

17.Measure the voltage between the No. 7 terminal of under-hood fuse/relay box connector E (16P) and body ground with the under-hood fuse/relay box connectors connected.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Repair open in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box and the fan control relay.n


NO - Repair open in the wire between the No. 30 fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and the under-hood fuse/relay box.n
