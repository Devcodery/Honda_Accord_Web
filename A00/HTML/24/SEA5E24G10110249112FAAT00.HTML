Radiator and A/C Condenser Fan Low Speed Circuit Troubleshooting

NOTE:

  • Do not use this troubleshooting procedure if the A/C compressor is inoperative. Refer to the symptom troubleshooting index.
  • Before performing symptom troubleshooting, check for powertrain DTCs.

1.Check the No. 9 (20 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box, and the No. 30 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.

Are the fuses OK?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Replace the fuse(s), and recheck.n


2.Remove the A/C condenser fan relay from the under-hood fuse/relay box, and test it.

Is the relay OK?


YES - Go to 3.


NO - Replace the  A/C condenser fan relay.n


3.Measure the voltage between the No. 1 terminal of the A/C condenser fan relay 4P socket and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 4.


NO - Replace the under-hood fuse/relay box.n

4.Connect the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals of the A/C condenser fan relay 4P socket with a jumper wire.

 

Does the A/C condenser fan run?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Go to 20.


5.Disconnect the jumper wire.

6.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

7.Measure the voltage between the No. 3 terminal of the A/C condenser fan relay 4P socket and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 8.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the No. 30 fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and the A/C condenser fan relay.n


8.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

9.Reinstall the A/C condenser fan relay.

10.Remove A/C diode A from the left side (LHD) or the right side (RHD) of the dashboard.

11.Check for current flow in both directions between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals of A/C diode A.

 

Is there current flow in only one direction?


YES - Go to 12.


NO - Replace A/C diode A.n


12.Make sure the engine coolant temperature is below 93 °C (199 °F).

13.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

14.Measure the voltage between the No. 2 terminal of A/C diode A 2P connector and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 15.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the A/C condenser fan relay and A/C diode A.n

15.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

16.Reinstall A/C diode A.

17.Disconnect climate control unit connector B (16P).

18.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

19.Measure the voltage between the No. 13 terminal of climate control unit connector B (16P) and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Check for loose wires or poor connections at climate control unit B (16P) and at A/C diode A 2P connector. If the connections are good, substitute a known-good climate control unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original  climate control unit.n


NO - Repair open in the wire between A/C diode A and the climate control unit.n


20.Disconnect the jumper wire.

21.Reinstall the A/C condenser fan relay.

22.Disconnect the A/C condenser fan 2P connector.

23.Turn the ignition switch ON (II), then turn the A/C and fan switches ON.

24.Measure the voltage between the No. 2 terminal of the A/C condenser fan 2P connector and body ground.

 


Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 25.


NO - Replace open in the wire between the A/C condenser fan relay and the A/C condenser fan.n


25.Turn the A/C and fan switches OFF, then turn the ignition switch OFF.

26.Reconnect the A/C condenser fan 2P connector.

27.Connect the No. 1 terminal of the A/C condenser fan 2P connector to body ground with jumper wire.

 

28.Turn the ignition switch ON (II), then turn the A/C and fan switches ON.

Does the A/C condenser fan run?


YES - Go to 29.


NO - Replace the A/C condenser fan motor.n


29.Turn the A/C and fan switches OFF, then turn the ignition switch OFF.

30.Disconnect the jumper wire.

31.Remove the fan control relay from the multi-relay box, and test it.

Is the relay OK?


YES - Go to 32.


NO - Replace the fan control relay.n


32.Turn the ignition switch ON (II), then turn the A/C and fan switches ON.

33.Measure the voltage between the No. 1 terminal of the fan control relay 5P socket and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 34.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the A/C condenser fan and the fan control relay.n


34.Turn the A/C and fan switches OFF, then turn the ignition switch OFF.

35.Disconnect the radiator fan 2P connector.

36.Check for continuity between the No. 4 terminal of the fan control relay 5P socket and the No. 2 terminal of the radiator fan 2P connector.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 37.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the fan control relay and the radiator fan.n


37.Check for continuity between the No. 1 terminal of the radiator fan 2P connector and body ground.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Replace the radiator fan motor.n


NO - Check for an open in the wire between the radiator fan and body ground. If the wire is OK, check for poor ground at G301.n
