Audio System Symptom Troubleshooting - No navigation sub display shown on the screen

NO navigation subdisplay shown on the screen


NOTE: Check that ''A/C mode'' is shown now on the display.

1.Repeat turning the ignition switch ON (II) and OFF to see if the problem appears.

Does the problem appear?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Operation is normal.n


2.Check the BUS communication DTC . Check to see if the entire LCD comes on, and goes out.

Does it come on and go out entirely?


YES - Faulty audio unit.n


NO - Go to 3.


3.Remove the center upper panel, and check that the audio unit connector C (10P) is correctly connected to the navigation subdisplay.

Is it normal?


YES - Go to 4.


NO - Reconnect the audio unit connector C (10P) and recheck the function. n

4.Measure voltage between No. 1 and No. 2 terminals of the subdisplay assembly 10P connector.

Is there 5 V?


YES - Faulty navigation subdisplay and audio unit.n


NO - Go to 5.


5.Measure voltage between No. 1 and No. 2 terminals of the audio unit connector C (10P).

Is there 5 V?


YES - Faulty navigation subdisplay and audio unit.n


NO - Faulty audio unit.n
