Brake Booster Replacement

LHD Model


1.Remove the master cylinder.

2.Disconnect the vacuum hose (A) from the brake booster (B).

 

3.Remove the hand primer bracket (A).

NOTE: Do not disconnect the hose with hand primer (B).

 

4.Remove the fuel filter bracket (A) from frame.

NOTE: For the fuel heater equipped model, remove the fuel heater with fuel filter bracket.

 

5.Remove the cable (A) from the body.

 

6.Remove the brake lines from the hose clamp (A).

 

7.Remove the air cleaner.

8.Remove the intake air duct.

9.Remove the lock pin (A) and the joint pin (B), then disconnect the yoke (C) from the brake pedal.

 

10.Remove the brake booster mounting nuts (D).

11.Remove the yoke from brake booster rod (E).

12.Move the brake booster (A) toward front and right side.



  • Be careful not to damage the booster surfaces and threads of the brake booster rod (B) and the booster stud bolts.
  • Be careful not to bend or damage the brake lines.



 

13.Turn the brake booster (A) until the brake booster rod (B) comes upward.

 

14.Remove the brake booster (A) from the engine compartment.

 

15.Install the brake booster in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Tighten all mounting hardware to the specified torque values.
  • Use a new lock pin whenever installing.
  • After installing the brake booster and master cylinder, fill the reservoir with new brake fluid, bleed the brake system, and adjust the brake pedal height and free play.
