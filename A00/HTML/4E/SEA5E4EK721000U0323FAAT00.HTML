DTC Troubleshooting: U0323 (184-7)

DTC U0323:  F-CAN Malfunction (Gauge Control Module)


1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

2.Clear the DTC with the HDS.

3.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

4.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

5.Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.

Is DTC U0323 indicated?


YES - Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the gauge control module, then recheck. If DTC U0323 is indicated, substitute a known-good gauge control module, then recheck. If DTC U0323 is not indicated, replace the original  gauge control module.n


NO - Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time.n
