Back-up Light Switch Test

1.Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the radio and the navigation system, then write down the frequencies for the radio preset buttons. Disconnect the negative (-) cable first, then the positive (+) cable from the battery. Remove the battery.

2.Remove the air cleaner housing.

3.Remove the battery base.

 

4.Remove the intake air cover.

5.Remove the resonator chamber.

 

6.Disconnect the back-up light switch connector (A).

 

7.Check for continuity between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals of the back-up light switch 2P connector. There should be continuity when the shift lever is in reverse.

8.If necessary, replace the  back-up light switch. Apply liquid gasket (P/N 08C70-K0234M) to the switch threads, and install the switch on the transmission housing. Tighten the back-up light switch to the specified torque.

9.Install the resonator chamber.

10.Install the battery base, then install the ground cable.

11.Install the air cleaner housing.

12.Install the battery. Connect the positive (+) cable first, then the negative (-) cable to the battery.

13.Enter the anti-theft codes for the radio and navigation system, then enter the customer's radio station presets.

14.Do the power window control unit reset procedure.
