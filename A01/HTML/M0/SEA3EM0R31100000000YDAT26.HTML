Rear Floor/Rear Floor Cross-member Removal

  • Cut and remove the rear floor.
  • Cut the rear floor 15 mm (0.6 in.) from the welded flange of the rear floor upper cross-member.

NOTE: When cutting the rear floor, take care not to damage the rear frame stiffener and rear wheelhouse.

  • Check the rear frame and rear floor cross-member position, and check for damage.
  • If necessary, replace the rear frame B and rear floor cross-member.
