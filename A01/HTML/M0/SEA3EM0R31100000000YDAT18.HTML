Roof Panel Installation

1.Clamp the new roof panel (A), and install the roof arch gusset (B) and rear pillar gusset (C). Check the body dimension, opening repair chart.
Check the width of the roof molding installation areas (D).

 

2.Tack weld the roof panel.

3.Temporarily install the windshield and tailgate, and check for differences in level and clearance.

4.Install the power tailgate drive unit (A), and check the operation of the tailgate.

 

5.Main weld the roof panel (A) and front roof rail (B).
