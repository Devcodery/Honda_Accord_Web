Front Pillar Outer Panel Removal

  • Cut and remove the wheelhouse upper member carefully so they can be reused.
  • When removing the wheelhouse upper member, do not damage the wheelhouse upper member separator.
  • Cut and pry off the front pillar outer panel.
  • Check the front pillar lower stiffener position, and check for damage. If necessary replace it.
  • When replacing the front pillar lower stiffener, remove the front pillar separator, and glue the urethane block at the separator mounted position.
  • If necessary, replace the reinforcement front extension.
